How do I word a thank-you note for a dinner where the host went all out?
A
A truly memorable thank-you note names one specific thing — the lamb, the candles, a particular conversation. Try:

"Dear Sarah — the lamb was extraordinary, but it was the way you drew everyone into the conversation that I'll remember most. Thank you for a perfect evening."
